About Elevencia Learning

Empowering International High School Learners

Elevencia Learning is the dedicated high school division of Torche Education (PT. Obor Pengetahuan Indonesia untuk Masyarakat), guiding students from foundational STEM clarity to A* Cambridge exam mastery.

Built on Torche Education's track record of mentoring thousands of engineering and science learners across Indonesia and abroad, Elevencia Learning delivers focused high school mentorship designed specifically for international curricula and bilingual fluency.

Official Cambridge IGCSE & A-Level Alignment

Structured around Cambridge 0580/0606/0625/0620/0610 (IGCSE) and 9709/9231/9702/9701/9700 (AS & A-Level) syllabi, complete with past-paper drilling and marking scheme insights.

100% Live Online with Full Recordings

Interactive 100-minute live sessions via Google Meet or Zoom with digital whiteboard annotations, Google Workspace materials, and complimentary session recordings.

Bilingual Instruction & Language Mastery

Learn seamlessly in English or Bahasa Indonesia for STEM subjects, or accelerate communication fluency through dedicated Academic English and Mandarin Chinese coaching.

Part of the Torche Education Ecosystem

Operated under PT. Obor Pengetahuan Indonesia untuk Masyarakat, Elevencia Learning connects high school students with Torche's experienced university STEM mentors, ensuring smooth academic readiness from IGCSE & A-Levels into global university STEM programs.

Learning Programs

Flexible Class Formats Built Around Your Goals

Whether you need weekly concept reinforcement, intensive past-paper exam prep, or fluent English and Mandarin communication, every session adapts to your school syllabus.

Lecturing & Concept Mastery Class

Chapter-by-chapter explanations of high school STEM topics with real-world engineering intuition, step-by-step worked examples, and interactive guided problem solving.

IGCSE & A-Level Exam Preparation

Targeted revision for May/June and Oct/Nov Cambridge exam series using timed past papers, examiner report pitfalls, structured mark-scheme phrasing, and Paper 5/6 practical skills.

Consultation & Homework Clinic

Student-driven sessions where you bring school worksheets, Internal Assessments, lab reports, or tricky topic questions for direct live troubleshooting with a subject mentor.

Global Language Fluency (English & Mandarin)

Accelerate English writing/speaking fluency (IGCSE 0500/0510 & IELTS) and Mandarin Chinese proficiency (IGCSE 0547 & HSK 1–5) through immersive communicative practice.

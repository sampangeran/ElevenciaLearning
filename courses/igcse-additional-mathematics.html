Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606)

Tailored for high-achieving Grade 9–10 students taking Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606). This course bridges GCSE-level algebra into AS/A-Level pure mathematics—covering functions, logarithms, polynomials, combinatorics, trigonometry identities, vectors, and single-variable calculus.

Primary Textbooks & Syllabus References:

  1. Pemberton, S. (2023). Cambridge IGCSE and O Level Additional Mathematics Coursebook (3rd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
  2. Haese, M., et al. (2022). Additional Mathematics for Cambridge IGCSE & O Level. Haese Mathematics.

Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Functions & Quadratic Inequalities
  • 2. Indices, Surds, Logarithms & Polynomials
  • 3. Permutations, Combinations & Binomial Theorem
  • 4. Circular Measure & Trigonometry
  • 5. Coordinate Geometry & Vectors
  • 6. Differentiation & Applications
  • 7. Integration & Kinematics
  • 8. Paper 1 & Paper 2 Exam Mastery

1. Functions & Quadratic Inequalities

Understand rigorous function definitions, discriminant conditions, and modulus graphs.

  1. One-to-one functions, domain/range restrictions, composite and inverse functions
  2. Completing the square and discriminant conditions (b² - 4ac)
  3. Solving quadratic and modulus equations & inequalities
  4. Sketching absolute value graphs |f(x)| and f(|x|)

2. Indices, Surds, Logarithms & Polynomials

Master algebraic manipulation of powers, logs, and higher-degree polynomials.

  1. Rationalizing surds and solving surd equations
  2. Laws of logarithms, change of base, and exponential equations
  3. Factor theorem, remainder theorem, and polynomial division
  4. Converting non-linear relationships to straight-line form (Y = mX + c)

3. Permutations, Combinations & Binomial Theorem

Solve counting problems with restrictions and expand binomial expressions.

  1. Permutations (nPr) with repetition, adjacent, and separated constraints
  2. Combinations (nCr) for committee and selection problems
  3. Binomial expansion for positive integer n and finding specific coefficients
  4. Arithmetic and geometric progressions including sum to infinity

4. Circular Measure & Trigonometry

Transition from degrees to radians and prove advanced trigonometric identities.

  1. Radian measure, arc length s = rθ, and sector/segment area
  2. Graphs of sin, cos, tan, cosec, sec, and cot with transformations
  3. Pythagorean identities (sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ)
  4. Solving trigonometric equations over multiple quadrants

5. Coordinate Geometry & Vectors

Combine geometric coordinate proofs with 2D vector kinematics.

  1. Perpendicular bisectors, area of rectilinear figures (shoelace method)
  2. Circle equations (x - a)² + (y - b)² = r² and tangent intersections
  3. Unit vectors, relative velocity, and constant-velocity particle interception

6. Differentiation & Applications

Learn first-principles differentiation techniques and optimization modeling.

  1. Product rule, quotient rule, and chain rule for algebraic, trig, exp, and log functions
  2. Equations of tangents and normals to curves
  3. Stationary points, second derivative test, and maxima/minima optimization
  4. Connected rates of change and small increments (δy ≈ dy/dx · δx)

7. Integration & Kinematics

Compute definite integrals, enclosed areas, and variable-acceleration motion.

  1. Indefinite and definite integration of polynomials, e^(ax+b), sin/cos, and 1/(ax+b)
  2. Area between a curve and axes or between two intersecting curves
  3. Displacement, velocity, and acceleration via calculus (s ↔ v ↔ a)

8. Paper 1 & Paper 2 Exam Mastery

Conquer the 2025+ non-calculator Paper 1 and calculator-allowed Paper 2 formats.

  1. Non-calculator exact surd, log, and trig speed drills for Paper 1
  2. Multi-step calculus & proof structure for Paper 2
  3. Past-paper timing and mark-scheme verification

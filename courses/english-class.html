Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Cambridge IGCSE First & Second Language Foundations
  • 2. Academic Reading Comprehension & Summary Writing
  • 3. Essay Writing, Directed Writing & Argumentation
  • 4. IELTS Academic Preparation (Band 7.0–8.5+)
  • 5. Speaking Fluency, Presentations & Interviews
  • 6. Literature Analysis & Critical Thinking

1. Cambridge IGCSE First & Second Language Foundations

Master core reading strategies, tone identification, and exam rubric expectations.

  1. Explicit vs implicit meaning inference and writer's effect analysis (0500 Paper 1)
  2. Skimming, scanning, and note-taking for IGCSE ESL (0510)
  3. Audience, register, and purpose adaptation (formal vs informal tone)
  4. Precision grammar, punctuation, and idiomatic academic collocations

2. Academic Reading Comprehension & Summary Writing

Synthesize dense non-fiction texts into concise, high-scoring summaries.

  1. Identifying core content points without copying source phrasing
  2. Paraphrasing techniques and cohesive linking devices
  3. Strict word-count control (120-word and 150-word summary drills)
  4. Analyzing persuasive devices, rhetoric, and imagery

3. Essay Writing, Directed Writing & Argumentation

Write compelling letters, reports, articles, speeches, and argumentative essays.

  1. Directed Writing formats: formal reports, speeches, magazine articles, and journals
  2. Descriptive & narrative composition with sensory language and pacing
  3. Structuring balanced discursive and persuasive thesis arguments
  4. Line-by-line essay feedback and vocabulary upgrading

4. IELTS Academic Preparation (Band 7.0–8.5+)

Strategic coaching across all four IELTS Academic modules for university applications.

  1. Task 1 data description (line graphs, bar/pie charts, maps, and process diagrams)
  2. Task 2 essay structures (opinion, discussion, advantages/disadvantages, problem-solution)
  3. Reading True/False/Not Given and heading matching strategies
  4. Listening map/multiple-choice traps and Speaking Part 1–3 fluency

5. Speaking Fluency, Presentations & Interviews

Speak naturally and persuasively in classrooms, debates, and scholarship interviews.

  1. Pronunciation clarity, intonation, and natural rhythm
  2. Structuring impromptu responses (PREP / STAR frameworks)
  3. Academic presentation delivery and Q&A defense
  4. Mock university admission and Model UN speaking drills

6. Literature Analysis & Critical Thinking

Develop sharp textual interpretation for IGCSE Literature and IB English essays.

  1. Unseen poetry and prose close-reading techniques
  2. Theme, character arc, and motif analysis with embedded quotations
  3. PEEL / PETAL analytical paragraph construction

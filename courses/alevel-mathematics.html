Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Pure Mathematics 1 (Paper 1)
  • 2. Pure Mathematics 2 & 3 — Algebra & Calculus (Paper 3)
  • 3. Pure Mathematics 3 — Differential Equations, Vectors & Complex Numbers
  • 4. Mechanics (Paper 4 / M1)
  • 5. Probability & Statistics 1 (Paper 5 / S1)
  • 6. Probability & Statistics 2 (Paper 6 / S2) & Exam Prep

1. Pure Mathematics 1 (Paper 1)

Master all foundational AS Pure Math topics required for Paper 1.

  1. Quadratics, completing the square, discriminant, and hidden quadratic substitutions
  2. Functions: domain, range, inverse, composite, and graph transformations
  3. Coordinate geometry of lines and circles
  4. Circular measure, trigonometric identities, and equations
  5. Binomial expansion (nCr), arithmetic & geometric progressions
  6. Differentiation (chain rule, stationary points, connected rates) and integration (definite areas, volumes of revolution)

2. Pure Mathematics 2 & 3 — Algebra & Calculus (Paper 3)

Conquer A2 logarithmic, trigonometric, and calculus techniques.

  1. Modulus functions, polynomial division, partial fractions, and generalized binomial series (1 + x)^n
  2. Logarithmic/exponential equations and compound-angle / R-formula (R cos(θ - α))
  3. Product, quotient, parametric, and implicit differentiation
  4. Integration by parts, substitution, partial fractions, and trapezium rule
  5. Numerical methods: change of sign and iterative root finding

3. Pure Mathematics 3 — Differential Equations, Vectors & Complex Numbers

Master the highest-weight A2 Paper 3 analytical topics.

  1. Separable first-order differential equations and modeling contexts
  2. 3D vector equations of lines, dot products, angle between lines, and skew lines
  3. Complex numbers: Argand diagrams, modulus-argument form, loci, and De Moivre roots

4. Mechanics (Paper 4 / M1)

Apply Newton's laws and calculus kinematics to forces, pulleys, and inclined planes.

  1. Kinematics graphs and SUVAT equations under uniform acceleration
  2. Force resolution, equilibrium, Lami's theorem, and limiting friction (F ≤ μR)
  3. Connected particles, pulleys on inclined planes, and Newton's 2nd & 3rd laws
  4. Momentum, impulse, work, kinetic/potential energy, and power (P = Fv)
  5. Variable-acceleration kinematics via differentiation and integration

5. Probability & Statistics 1 (Paper 5 / S1)

Analyze data distributions, combinatorics, and discrete/normal probability models.

  1. Stem-and-leaf, box-and-whisker, histograms, variance, and coded mean/SD
  2. Permutations and combinations with restrictions
  3. Probability trees, conditional probability, and discrete random variables
  4. Binomial distribution B(n, p), geometric distribution Geo(p), and normal distribution N(μ, σ²)
  5. Normal approximation to the binomial with continuity correction

6. Probability & Statistics 2 (Paper 6 / S2) & Exam Prep

Complete A2 statistical inference and timed past-paper drilling.

  1. Poisson distribution Po(λ) and Poisson/Normal approximations
  2. Linear combinations of independent normal random variables
  3. Continuous random variables: PDF, CDF, E(X), Var(X), and median
  4. Central Limit Theorem, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing (Type I & Type II errors)
  5. Timed Paper 1/3/4/5/6 mock practice and examiner report pitfalls

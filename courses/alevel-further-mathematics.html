Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Further Pure Mathematics 1 — Roots, Series & Induction
  • 2. Further Pure Mathematics 1 — Matrices, Vectors & Polar Coordinates
  • 3. Further Pure Mathematics 2 (Paper 2)
  • 4. Further Mechanics (Paper 3)
  • 5. Further Probability & Statistics (Paper 4)
  • 6. Proof Structure & Past-Paper Mastery

1. Further Pure Mathematics 1 — Roots, Series & Induction

Master algebraic proofs, telescoping series, and polynomial root transformations.

  1. Roots of quadratic, cubic, and quartic equations (Vieta's formulas & substitutions)
  2. Rational functions, oblique asymptotes, and inequalities
  3. Summation of finite series via method of differences and standard ∑r², ∑r³ formulas
  4. Mathematical induction proofs for series, divisibility, and matrices

2. Further Pure Mathematics 1 — Matrices, Vectors & Polar Coordinates

Build geometric intuition across linear transformations and polar curves.

  1. 2x2 and 3x3 matrices, determinants, inverses, invariant lines, and geometric transformations
  2. 3D vector cross product, scalar triple product, equation of a plane, and shortest distances
  3. Polar coordinates (r, θ): sketching roses/cardioids and area = ½∫r² dθ

3. Further Pure Mathematics 2 (Paper 2)

Dive into hyperbolic functions, eigenvalues, Maclaurin expansions, and 2nd-order ODEs.

  1. Hyperbolic functions (sinh, cosh, tanh), Osborn's rule, and logarithmic inverse forms
  2. Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization of matrices + Cayley-Hamilton theorem
  3. 1st-order integrating factor & 2nd-order linear differential equations (complementary + particular integral)
  4. De Moivre's theorem, summation of trig series, and Maclaurin series + reduction formulas

4. Further Mechanics (Paper 3)

Solve multi-dimensional mechanics involving projectiles, collisions, and rigid dynamics.

  1. Projectile motion on horizontal and inclined planes (equation of trajectory)
  2. Equilibrium of rigid bodies, toppling vs sliding, and Hooke's law elastic strings/springs
  3. Circular motion in horizontal and vertical circles (loss of contact / slack string)
  4. Oblique and direct collisions (Newton's experimental law & coefficient of restitution e)
  5. Motion under variable forces using differential equations

5. Further Probability & Statistics (Paper 4)

Apply continuous distributions, generating functions, and non-parametric hypothesis tests.

  1. Continuous random variables, cumulative distribution functions, and functions of RVs (Y = g(X))
  2. Confidence intervals and t-distribution / paired-sample & two-sample t-tests
  3. Chi-squared (χ²) goodness-of-fit and contingency table tests
  4. Non-parametric tests: single-sample sign test, Wilcoxon signed-rank & rank-sum tests
  5. Probability Generating Functions (PGFs): G_X(t), mean, variance, and sum of RVs

6. Proof Structure & Past-Paper Mastery

Develop university-grade rigor and speed across 9231 Papers 1–4.

  1. Structuring airtight induction and vector-plane proofs
  2. Avoiding sign and boundary errors in 2nd-order ODEs and polar integrals
  3. Full timed mock reviews of recent 9231 exam series

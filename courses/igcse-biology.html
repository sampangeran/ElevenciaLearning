Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Characteristics, Cells & Biological Molecules
  • 2. Enzymes, Plant Nutrition & Transport
  • 3. Human Nutrition, Respiration & Gas Exchange
  • 4. Coordination, Homeostasis & Excretion
  • 5. Reproduction, Inheritance & Biotechnology
  • 6. Organisms, Ecosystems & Human Influences
  • 7. Paper 6 (ATP) & Mark-Scheme Mastery

1. Characteristics, Cells & Biological Molecules

Explore microscopy, cell specialization, membrane transport, and food tests.

  1. MRS GREN classification and dichotomous keys
  2. Plant, animal, and bacterial cell ultrastructure + magnification formula (M = I/A)
  3. Diffusion, osmosis (water potential, turgid/plasmolyzed), and active transport
  4. Carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, DNA base pairing, and Benedict's/Biuret/DCPIP tests

2. Enzymes, Plant Nutrition & Transport

Connect enzyme lock-and-key kinetics with leaf structure, photosynthesis, and transpiration.

  1. Enzyme specificity, temperature/pH denaturation, and limiting factor graphs
  2. Photosynthesis equation, leaf cross-section adaptations, and mineral ions (Mg²⁺, NO₃⁻)
  3. Xylem vs phloem structure, transpiration stream, potometer experiments, and translocation

3. Human Nutrition, Respiration & Gas Exchange

Follow digestion, circulatory anatomy, immunity, and aerobic/anaerobic respiration.

  1. Alimentary canal, mechanical/chemical digestion, villi adaptations, and bile
  2. Double circulation, heart valves, arteries/veins/capillaries, and coronary heart disease
  3. Pathogens, active vs passive immunity, lymphocytes, and vaccination
  4. Alveolar gas exchange, breathing mechanics, and oxygen debt after anaerobic respiration

4. Coordination, Homeostasis & Excretion

Understand nervous reflexes, eye accommodation, hormones, kidney nephrons, and thermoregulation.

  1. Sensory, relay, and motor neurones, reflex arcs, synapses, and eye focusing
  2. Adrenaline, insulin/glucagon blood glucose control, and auxin plant tropisms
  3. Kidney ultrafiltration, selective reabsorption in nephrons, and urea excretion
  4. Skin vasodilation/vasoconstriction and negative feedback loops

5. Reproduction, Inheritance & Biotechnology

Master mitosis, meiosis, monohybrid inheritance, selection, and genetic modification.

  1. Asexual vs sexual reproduction, flower pollination, human menstrual hormones (FSH/LH/estrogen/progesterone)
  2. Mitosis vs meiosis, DNA transcription/translation to protein
  3. Punnett squares, codominance, ABO blood groups, and sex-linked inheritance
  4. Natural vs artificial selection, bacterial plasmids, insulin synthesis, and fermenters

6. Organisms, Ecosystems & Human Influences

Analyze energy flow, nutrient cycles, population curves, and conservation.

  1. Food webs, pyramids of biomass/energy, carbon cycle, and nitrogen cycle (nitrification/denitrification)
  2. Sigmoid population growth curves (lag, log, stationary, death)
  3. Eutrophication, deforestation, greenhouse gases, and sustainable fisheries

7. Paper 6 (ATP) & Mark-Scheme Mastery

Master biological drawing proportions, percentage change calculations, and Paper 6 experiments.

  1. Biological drawing rules (sharp lines, no shading, >50% space) and magnification
  2. Designing valid enzyme/osmosis/photosynthesis investigations with controls
  3. Command word discipline: State vs Describe vs Explain vs Suggest

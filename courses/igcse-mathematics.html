Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580 / 0607)

Designed for Grade 9–10 students preparing for Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580) or International Mathematics (0607). The course builds deep mastery across algebraic manipulation, coordinate geometry, functions, trigonometry, vectors, and probability while training speed and accuracy for Paper 2 (Short-Answer) and Paper 4 (Extended Structured Questions).

Primary Textbooks & Syllabus References:

  1. Pemberton, S. (2023). Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics Core and Extended Coursebook (3rd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
  2. Pimentel, R., & Wall, T. (2023). Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics Core and Extended (5th ed.). Hodder Education.

Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Number, Ratio & Arithmetic Mastery
  • 2. Algebra, Equations & Inequalities
  • 3. Functions & Coordinate Geometry
  • 4. Geometry, Circle Theorems & Constructions
  • 5. Mensuration & 2D/3D Trigonometry
  • 6. Vectors & Transformations
  • 7. Probability & Statistics
  • 8. Paper 2 & Paper 4 Exam Mastery

1. Number, Ratio & Arithmetic Mastery

Strengthen numerical fluency, upper/lower bounds, and financial/compound calculations tested across Paper 2 and Paper 4.

  1. Number types, standard form, and exact surd/fractional arithmetic
  2. Percentages, compound interest, reverse percentages, and exponential growth/decay
  3. Direct and inverse proportion, rates, and currency/unit conversions
  4. Upper and lower bounds in real-world measurement problems
  5. Set notation and Venn diagrams with three sets

2. Algebra, Equations & Inequalities

Build clean, error-free algebraic manipulation skills for quadratic, simultaneous, and fractional equations.

  1. Expanding, factorizing quadratics, difference of squares, and completing the square
  2. Linear, quadratic, and non-linear simultaneous equations
  3. Algebraic fractions and changing the subject of complex formulae
  4. Linear inequalities and shading feasible regions on graphs
  5. Sequences: nth term of linear, quadratic, cubic, and geometric progressions

3. Functions & Coordinate Geometry

Connect algebraic expressions with visual graphs, gradients, and function transformations.

  1. Domain, range, composite functions fg(x), and inverse functions f⁻¹(x)
  2. Gradient, midpoint, distance, perpendicular and parallel lines
  3. Sketching linear, quadratic, cubic, reciprocal, and exponential curves
  4. Estimating gradients using tangents and solving equations graphically
  5. Linear programming and graphical inequalities

4. Geometry, Circle Theorems & Constructions

Master angle reasoning, similarity, and official Cambridge geometrical proof language.

  1. Parallel lines, polygons, symmetry, and angle properties
  2. Congruency (SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS) and similarity in lengths, areas, and volumes
  3. All circle theorems: cyclic quadrilaterals, alternate segment theorem, and tangents
  4. Loci and scale drawings with compasses and protractors

5. Mensuration & 2D/3D Trigonometry

Solve multi-step spatial geometry problems in two and three dimensions.

  1. Arc length, sector area, prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, and frustums
  2. Right-angled trigonometry (SOHCAHTOA) and Pythagoras' theorem
  3. Sine rule, cosine rule, ambiguous case, and area = ½ab sin C
  4. Bearings, angles of elevation/depression, and 3D trigonometry
  5. Exact trigonometric values and trigonometric equations (0°–360°)

6. Vectors & Transformations

Apply column vectors, position vectors, and geometric transformations cleanly.

  1. Vector addition, scalar multiplication, magnitude, and geometric vector proofs
  2. Collinearity and ratio division on vector diagrams
  3. Reflection, rotation, translation, and enlargement (including negative/fractional scale factors)
  4. Combined transformations and describing single transformations precisely

7. Probability & Statistics

Analyze datasets, cumulative frequency, histograms, and conditional probability.

  1. Mean, median, mode, range, and grouped frequency estimates
  2. Histograms with unequal class widths and frequency density
  3. Cumulative frequency diagrams, quartiles, and interquartile range
  4. Tree diagrams, conditional probability without replacement, and Venn probability

8. Paper 2 & Paper 4 Exam Mastery

Timed past-paper simulations targeting non-calculator fluency and structured Extended questions.

  1. Paper 2 non-calculator accuracy and time-budgeting drills (2025+ syllabus update)
  2. Paper 4 multi-part problem decomposition and show-that proofs
  3. Rounding rules (3 significant figures, 1 decimal place for angles)
  4. Common examiner report pitfalls and mark-scheme method marks (M1/A1)

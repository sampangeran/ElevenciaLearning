Cambridge IGCSE Physics (0625)

Build strong physical intuition and mathematical precision for Cambridge IGCSE Physics (0625). Covers Mechanics, Thermal Physics, Waves, Electricity & Magnetism, Nuclear Physics, and the updated Space Physics unit, plus dedicated Paper 2 (MCQ), Paper 4 (Extended Theory), and Paper 6 (Alternative to Practical) training.

Primary Textbooks & Syllabus References:

  1. Sang, D., et al. (2023). Cambridge IGCSE Physics Coursebook (3rd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
  2. Duncan, T., & Kennett, H. (2023). Cambridge IGCSE Physics (4th ed.). Hodder Education.

Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Motion, Forces & Energy
  • 2. Thermal Physics
  • 3. Waves, Light & Sound
  • 4. Electricity & Magnetism
  • 5. Nuclear Physics
  • 6. Space Physics (Updated Syllabus)
  • 7. Paper 6 (ATP) & Extended Theory Mastery

1. Motion, Forces & Energy

Master kinematics graphs, Newton's laws, momentum, moments, and energy conservation.

  1. Speed-time and distance-time graphs, uniform acceleration, and free fall
  2. Newton's laws, terminal velocity, circular motion, and Hooke's law
  3. Principle of moments, centre of gravity, and vector addition of forces
  4. Impulse, conservation of momentum, kinetic/potential energy, work, and power
  5. Pressure in solids and liquids (p = ρgh)

2. Thermal Physics

Link kinetic particle theory with gas laws, thermal expansion, and heat transfer.

  1. Kinetic molecular model of solids, liquids, and gases; Brownian motion
  2. Boyle's law (pV = constant) and Kelvin temperature scale
  3. Specific heat capacity (E = mcΔT), evaporation, and cooling curves
  4. Conduction, convection, and thermal radiation experiments

3. Waves, Light & Sound

Analyze wave equations, reflection/refraction, lenses, and the electromagnetic spectrum.

  1. Transverse vs longitudinal waves, v = fλ, reflection, refraction, and diffraction
  2. Snell's law (n = sin i / sin r), total internal reflection, and optical fibres
  3. Thin converging lenses: ray diagrams, magnification, and real/virtual images
  4. Electromagnetic spectrum applications and sound/ultrasound echoes

4. Electricity & Magnetism

Design and solve D.C. circuit networks, transformers, and electromagnetic induction problems.

  1. Electrostatics, current I = Q/t, EMF, potential difference, and Ohm's law
  2. Series and parallel circuits, potential dividers, LDRs, and thermistors
  3. Electrical power (P = IV), energy (E = IVt), fuses, and circuit safety
  4. Magnetic fields, motor effect (Fleming's left-hand rule), generators, and transformers (Vp/Vs = Np/Ns)

5. Nuclear Physics

Understand atomic structure, radioactive decay, half-life curves, and nuclear energy.

  1. Rutherford alpha-scattering experiment and proton/nucleon notation
  2. Alpha, beta, and gamma emissions: properties, deflection, and nuclear equations
  3. Half-life graphs with background radiation correction
  4. Nuclear fission, nuclear fusion, and radiation safety

6. Space Physics (Updated Syllabus)

Explore orbital mechanics, stellar evolution, Hubble's law, and the Big Bang.

  1. Earth's seasons, moon phases, solar system formation, and orbital speed (v = 2πr/T)
  2. Life cycle of low-mass and massive stars (nebula to black hole/white dwarf)
  3. Redshift, Cosmic Microwave Background Radiation (CMBR), and Hubble constant H₀

7. Paper 6 (ATP) & Extended Theory Mastery

Secure top marks in experimental design, error analysis, and Paper 4 phrasing.

  1. Planning experiments: independent/dependent/control variables & precaution tables
  2. Plotting best-fit graphs, gradient triangles, and justification of equality
  3. Exact definition flashcards and 3-step calculation layouts for Paper 4

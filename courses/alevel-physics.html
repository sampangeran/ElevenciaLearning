Cambridge International AS & A-Level Physics (9702)

Master Cambridge International AS & A-Level Physics (9702) from physical measurements and mechanics through fields, electromagnetism, quantum physics, and medical imaging. Includes step-by-step mathematical derivations plus Paper 3 (Practical) and Paper 5 (Planning, Analysis & Evaluation) error-bar coaching.

Primary Textbooks & Syllabus References:

  1. Sang, D., Jones, G., Chadha, G., & Woodside, R. (2020). Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics Coursebook (3rd ed.). Cambridge University Press.
  2. Mee, C., Crundell, M., Arnold, B., & Brown, W. (2020). Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics (3rd ed.). Hodder Education.

Course Syllabus & Learning Modules

Select any module below to explore topics covered in Lecturing and Exam Preparation sessions. Topics can also be customized to match your school's current chapter.

  • 1. Physical Quantities, Kinematics & Dynamics
  • 2. Forces, Work, Energy & Deformation of Solids
  • 3. Waves, Superposition & Stationary Waves
  • 4. Electricity, D.C. Circuits & Particle Physics
  • 5. Circular Motion, Gravitational & Electric Fields
  • 6. Capacitance, Electromagnetism & Alternating Currents
  • 7. Quantum Physics, Nuclear & Medical Imaging
  • 8. Thermodynamics, Oscillations, Astronomy & Paper 5

1. Physical Quantities, Kinematics & Dynamics

Develop rigorous vector uncertainty propagation and Newtonian mechanics.

  1. SI base units, homogeneity checks, random/systematic errors, and uncertainty propagation
  2. Projectile motion, drag forces, and kinematic graph calculus
  3. Momentum conservation in 1D/2D elastic and inelastic collisions

2. Forces, Work, Energy & Deformation of Solids

Analyze torque, hydrostatic pressure, Young modulus, and strain energy.

  1. Couple, torque, equilibrium of coplanar forces, and upthrust (Archimedes' principle)
  2. Efficiency, potential/kinetic energy conservation, and power P = Fv
  3. Stress, strain, Young modulus, and elastic vs plastic deformation

3. Waves, Superposition & Stationary Waves

Understand interference, diffraction gratings, polarization, and Doppler shifts.

  1. Progressive waves, intensity I ∝ A², Malus's law polarization, and Doppler effect
  2. Principle of superposition, double-slit interference (λ = ax/D), and diffraction grating (d sin θ = nλ)
  3. Stationary waves in stretched strings and open/closed air columns

4. Electricity, D.C. Circuits & Particle Physics

Master Kirchhoff's laws, potential dividers, and standard-model quark composition.

  1. Drift velocity (I = Anvq), I-V characteristics, resistivity, and internal resistance
  2. Kirchhoff's 1st & 2nd laws, potential dividers, and null-balance potentiometers
  3. Alpha/beta+/beta- decay, neutrinos, hadrons, baryons, mesons, and quark flavors

5. Circular Motion, Gravitational & Electric Fields

Connect radial centripetal dynamics with inverse-square field potentials.

  1. Angular velocity, centripetal force (mv²/r), and geostationary satellite orbits
  2. Newton's law of gravitation, gravitational potential φ = -GM/r, and escape velocity
  3. Coulomb's law, electric field strength E = -dV/dr, and uniform electric plates

6. Capacitance, Electromagnetism & Alternating Currents

Solve RC exponential decay, magnetic flux linkage, Hall voltage, and rectification.

  1. Capacitor networks, energy stored (½CV²), and exponential charging/discharging (τ = RC)
  2. Magnetic force on current/charges (F = BIL, F = Bqv), velocity selector, and Hall effect
  3. Faraday's & Lenz's laws, AC RMS values, full-wave rectification, and smoothing

7. Quantum Physics, Nuclear & Medical Imaging

Explore wave-particle duality, binding energy, X-rays, ultrasound, and PET scans.

  1. Photoelectric effect (hf = Φ + ½mv²_max), de Broglie wavelength, and atomic line spectra
  2. Mass defect, binding energy per nucleon, radioactive decay constant λ, and activity A = λN
  3. Ultrasound acoustic impedance (Z = ρc), X-ray attenuation (I = I₀e^-μx), CT & PET tracers

8. Thermodynamics, Oscillations, Astronomy & Paper 5

Complete thermal ideal gases, SHM, cosmology, and Paper 5 planning/error-bar analysis.

  1. First law of thermodynamics (ΔU = q + w), ideal gas PV = nRT, and kinetic theory pV = ⅓Nm<c²>
  2. Simple harmonic motion (a = -ω²x), resonance, and damping
  3. Luminosity, Stefan-Boltzmann law, Wien's law, Hubble's law, and Big Bang cosmology
  4. Paper 5 experimental planning, linearization, error bars, and worst-acceptable-fit lines
